What it is

Not another AI tool.
A team that belongs to your business.

EML AI Team is a complete, custom AI team for businesses with a team and work that keeps piling up. Every AI employee has a name, a role and a task, and works in your company's tone.

01

AI employees per department

Marketing, sales and follow-up, planning, admin and customer contact. A few agents per department, each doing one task well.

02

You steer via Telegram

All agents sit in one Telegram group. You hand out tasks by text or voice message, and you approve what matters.

03

A Chief of Staff

Sums up what happened and puts the decisions to you: approve or wait. From the Growth package.

04

A dashboard

What the agents did, your figures, your pipeline and your departments on one screen. From the Growth package.

05

Connected to your systems

Your customer records, mailbox, calendar, quotes, reviews and accounting. Which connections you get depends on your package.

No calling agents: your AI team works with text, not on the phone.

Terms

What you should know upfront.
In black and white.

  1. 01

    12-month contract

    Every package has a fixed 12-month contract. EML AI Team is not cancellable monthly.

  2. 02

    Setup in two parts

    You pay 50% of the setup at the start and 50% at go-live. The monthly fee starts at go-live.

  3. 03

    After 12 months

    The contract is renewed. That is needed to keep your system live.

  4. 04

    What the monthly fee covers

    Keeping your system live and working: hosting and server management, maintenance and updates, monitoring, support and fixing problems.

  5. 05

    What is not included

    Claude usage: you pay that directly to Anthropic, through your own account. Subscriptions to your own tools are also yours to pay.

  6. 06

    Want something extra

    New agents, connections or improvements are not part of the monthly fee. If you want something extra, we make a proposal with a one-off setup price, or you move to another package.

  7. 07

    Your data

    Your AI team runs on a dedicated server that we manage. Every contract comes with a data processing agreement.

  8. 08

    Prices

    All prices exclude VAT. Invoices are payable within 14 days.
