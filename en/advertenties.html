Who it's for

Is it right for your business?

It fits if

  • People actively search for what you do: a painter, a hairdresser, a physiotherapist, a garage
  • You can follow up new enquiries quickly, even when it's busy
  • You want to know what every euro spent on ads brings in

It doesn't fit (yet) if

  • You don't have time to follow up new enquiries
  • You want a guaranteed number of customers up front
  • You want to advertise prescription medicines: Google doesn't allow that in Belgium
